Privacy Policy – Archery Bounce: Bow & Arrow

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Archery Bounce: Bow & Arrow ("the game") is provided by LykioStudio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any account. The game does not require sign-in.

Your game progress and settings (levels, sound and other options) are stored only on your device.

The game uses the following third-party services, which automatically collect some information from your device:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language and country/region.
  • Network information: IP address and network type.
  • Identifiers: the Open Anonymous Device Identifier (OAID) used for advertising and the anonymous application ID (AAID) used for analytics.
  • App usage information: app launches, levels played, session length and interactions with ads.

2. How we use the information

  • To run the game properly on your device and keep it stable.
  • To show in-app advertisements (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads) and measure their performance.
  • To understand how players use the game and improve it (anonymous statistics).

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

HUAWEI Ads Kit (Huawei Software Technologies Co., Ltd.) – displays ads. With your consent, ads may be personalized based on your OAID and your interactions with ads. Privacy policy: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/

HUAWEI Analytics Kit (Huawei Software Technologies Co., Ltd.) – collects anonymous usage statistics. Privacy policy: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/

The information collected by these services is processed on Huawei servers in accordance with Huawei's own privacy policies. We do not operate our own servers and do not store your information ourselves.

4. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: you can turn off personalized ads or reset your OAID at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy on your HUAWEI device.
  • Deleting game data: go to Settings > Apps > Archery Bounce > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • You can contact us at any time to ask about, or request deletion of, information related to you.

5. Data retention

Information is kept only for the shortest period necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
